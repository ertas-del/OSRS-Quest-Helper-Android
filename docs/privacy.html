Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Quest Overlay is a free quest guide for Old School RuneScape on Android. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer never receives any of your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your quest progress, ticked items, overlay position and opacity.
  • Your travel settings, including what you've built in your house.
  • Your RuneScape name, levels and finished quests, if you choose to load them.

All of this is stored only in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone, and only when you ask

  • Load levels sends your RuneScape name to Jagex's official Old School hiscores to fetch your public skill levels. Jagex's privacy notice applies.
  • Import quests sends your RuneScape name to the Old School RuneScape Wiki's WikiSync service to fetch the quests you've finished. This only has data if you've used RuneLite's WikiSync plugin.
  • Map and Wiki buttons open a web page in your browser. What happens there is covered by that website's own policy.

Nothing is sent in the background, and nothing is sent to the developer.

What it never does

  • Read the game, its memory, its network traffic or your screen.
  • Ask for your password or log in to any account.
  • Track you, show ads, or share or sell data.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps, to show the quest card on top of the game.
  • Notifications, for the small notification that keeps the overlay running and lets you stop it.
  • Internet, only for the hiscores and WikiSync lookups above.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub.
