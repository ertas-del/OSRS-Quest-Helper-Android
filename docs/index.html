Quest Overlay

A quest guide that floats on top of Old School RuneScape on your Android phone. Every quest, one step at a time, with the route there and the items to bring.

Download APK

Free. No ads, no account.

Android 11 or newer. iPhones don't let apps draw over games.

Cook's Assistant

Try it: this is the real first quest from the app. Tap Done to tick steps off.

Install in three steps

  1. 1
    Download the APK on your phone.

    Tap Download APK above. Your browser may ask if you want to keep the file; say yes.

  2. 2
    Open it and allow the install.

    Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time. Play Protect may warn that the app isn't from the Play Store; choose Install anyway.

  3. 3
    Let it draw over other apps.

    Open Quest Overlay, tap Grant permission, and switch on Allow display over other apps. Then pick a quest, tap Start overlay, and open the game.

What it does

Every quest and miniquest

Over 200 quests, step by step, kept in sync with the RuneLite Quest Helper plugin. Your place is saved, even if you close the game.

The fastest way there

A route planner on your phone picks teleports, fairy rings, spirit trees, gliders, boats and shortcuts you can actually use.

Break a Teleport to House tablet, then the Kharyrll portal in your house.

Your house counts

Tell it about your portal nexus, jewellery box, mounted glory or garden fairy ring and routes go through your POH.

Puzzle help

Solvers and answers for quest puzzles, worked with taps only, so you never need the keyboard over the game.

Items and chat options

What to bring for each step, a full checklist for the quest, and the dialogue options to pick.

Knows your account

Load your levels from the official hiscores and your finished quests from WikiSync, so routes skip what you can't use yet.

Why it can't get you banned

It does

  • Draw a card on top of the screen, like any chat-head app
  • Wait for you to tap Done before moving on
  • Look up your levels on the public hiscores, only when you ask

It never

  • Reads the game, its memory or its network traffic
  • Taps, swipes or types for you
  • Asks for your password or logs in to anything

It works like having the wiki open in another window. The code is open on GitHub for anyone to check.
